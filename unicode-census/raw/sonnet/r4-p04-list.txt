Switch to LED bulbs; they use up to 80% less energy than incandescent ones.
Unplug chargers and electronics when not in use to avoid phantom power draw.
Set your thermostat a few degrees lower in winter and higher in summer.
Wash clothes in cold water and only run full loads.
Air-dry laundry instead of using a dryer whenever possible.
Seal drafts around windows and doors with weatherstripping.
Use a programmable or smart thermostat to avoid heating or cooling an empty house.
Run dishwashers only when full and skip the heated dry cycle.
Install low-flow showerheads to cut hot water usage.
Have your HVAC system serviced annually to keep it running efficiently.
