Set your thermostat a degree or two lower in winter and higher in summer.
Switch to LED bulbs throughout the house.
Unplug chargers and electronics when they're not actively in use.
Wash clothes in cold water and air-dry when possible.
Seal drafts around windows and doors with weatherstripping.
Run dishwashers and washing machines only with full loads.
Use a programmable or smart thermostat to automate temperature setbacks.
Install low-flow showerheads to cut water-heating costs.
Keep refrigerator coils clean and don't overfill the freezer.
Add insulation to your attic to reduce heating and cooling loss.
