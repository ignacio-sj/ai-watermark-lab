Install a programmable thermostat to automatically adjust temperature based on your schedule and preferences.
Seal air leaks around windows, doors, and ducts to prevent conditioned air from escaping your home.
Replace incandescent bulbs with LED lighting, which uses approximately 75% less energy.
Improve home insulation in attics, basements, and walls to reduce heating and cooling needs.
Run full loads in your dishwasher and washing machine rather than partial loads whenever possible.
Use cold water for laundry and adjust water heater temperature to 120°F to save energy on water heating.
Install window treatments like thermal curtains to reduce heat loss in winter and heat gain in summer.
Unplug devices and chargers when not in use, or use power strips to eliminate phantom power drain.
Maintain regular HVAC maintenance including filter changes to keep your system running efficiently.
Consider upgrading to ENERGY STAR certified appliances when replacements are needed.
