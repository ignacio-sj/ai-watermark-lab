Install a programmable thermostat to automatically adjust temperature based on occupancy and time of day.
Use LED bulbs instead of incandescent or CFL bulbs throughout your home for dramatically lower energy consumption.
Seal air leaks around windows and doors with weatherstripping or caulk to prevent heated or cooled air from escaping.
Unplug devices and chargers when not in use or use power strips to eliminate phantom power drain.
Run dishwasher and laundry machines with full loads only to maximize water and energy efficiency.
Upgrade to ENERGY STAR certified appliances when replacing old ones to reduce long-term energy consumption.
Improve insulation in your attic and walls to reduce heating and cooling needs throughout the year.
Use cold water for laundry when possible instead of hot water, which accounts for significant energy use.
Clean or replace HVAC filters regularly to ensure your heating and cooling system operates at peak efficiency.
Install window coverings or shade trees to reduce heat gain in summer and heat loss in winter.
