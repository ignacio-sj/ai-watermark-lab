Wash clothes in cold water and skip the dryer when weather allows.
Seal gaps around doors, windows, and outlets with weatherstripping or caulk.
Set the thermostat a couple of degrees lower in winter and higher in summer.
Replace remaining incandescent and halogen bulbs with LEDs.
Lower the water heater to about 120°F (49°C) and insulate the first few feet of pipe.
Run dishwashers and washing machines only with full loads.
Unplug chargers, game consoles, and set-top boxes, or put them on a switched power strip.
Clean or replace HVAC filters every one to three months.
Close blinds on sunny summer afternoons and open them on sunny winter days.
Take shorter showers and fit a low-flow showerhead to cut water heating costs.
