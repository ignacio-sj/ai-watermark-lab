Lower the thermostat a few degrees in winter and raise it in summer; each degree is roughly 1-3% off your heating or cooling bill.
Wash clothes in cold water, since about 90% of a washing machine's energy goes to heating the water.
Seal drafts around doors, windows, and outlets with weatherstripping and caulk.
Replace any remaining incandescent or halogen bulbs with LEDs.
Run the dishwasher and laundry only with full loads, and skip the heated dry cycle.
Unplug or use a switched power strip for devices that draw standby power, especially entertainment systems and chargers.
Clean or replace HVAC filters every one to three months so the system does not work harder than it needs to.
Add insulation in the attic, which is usually the cheapest place to get the biggest return.
Set the water heater to 120°F (49°C) and insulate the tank and the first few feet of pipe.
Use curtains deliberately: close them against summer sun and open them for winter sun on south-facing windows.
