Seal drafts around doors and windows with weatherstripping and caulk.
Lower the thermostat a few degrees in winter and raise it in summer.
Wash laundry in cold water and run only full loads.
Air-dry clothes on a rack or line instead of using the dryer.
Replace remaining incandescent and halogen bulbs with LEDs.
Unplug chargers, consoles, and idle electronics, or put them on a switched power strip.
Add insulation in the attic, the cheapest place to get the biggest heating return.
Set the water heater to about 120°F (49°C) and insulate the tank and first metres of pipe.
Clean or replace HVAC filters every one to three months so the system does not overwork.
Cook with a microwave, kettle, or air fryer rather than heating a full oven for small meals.
