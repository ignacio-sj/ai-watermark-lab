1. Replace incandescent and halogen bulbs with LEDs throughout the house.
2. Lower the thermostat a degree or two in winter and raise it in summer; a programmable thermostat makes this automatic.
3. Wash clothes in cold water and only run full loads.
4. Air-dry laundry instead of using the tumble dryer whenever weather and space allow.
5. Plug electronics into a switched power strip and turn it off to eliminate standby draw.
6. Seal gaps around windows, doors and outlets with weatherstripping or caulk.
7. Run the dishwasher only when full and skip the heated dry cycle.
8. Set the water heater to 120°F (49°C) and insulate exposed hot-water pipes.
9. Cook with lids on pots, match pan size to the burner, and use a microwave or toaster oven for small meals.
10. Clean or replace HVAC filters every few months so the system does not work harder than necessary.
