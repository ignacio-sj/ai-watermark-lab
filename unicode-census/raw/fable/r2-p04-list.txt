1. Replace incandescent and halogen bulbs with LEDs, which use about 75% less electricity.
2. Lower the thermostat one or two degrees in winter and raise it in summer; each degree saves roughly 3% on heating or cooling.
3. Seal drafts around windows, doors, and outlets with weatherstripping or caulk.
4. Wash clothes in cold water and hang them to dry whenever you can.
5. Put TVs, consoles, and chargers on a switched power strip to cut standby draw.
6. Run the dishwasher and washing machine only with full loads.
7. Set the water heater to 120°F (49°C) and insulate the tank and the first metre of hot pipe.
8. Use a programmable or smart thermostat so heating and cooling drop automatically when you are asleep or out.
9. Keep refrigerator coils clean and door seals tight, and do not set it colder than 37°F (3°C).
10. Close blinds on hot afternoons and open them on sunny winter days to manage solar heat.
