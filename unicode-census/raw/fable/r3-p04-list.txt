1. Lower your thermostat by 1 to 2 degrees in winter and raise it by the same in summer.
2. Replace remaining incandescent or halogen bulbs with LEDs.
3. Wash clothes in cold water and only run full loads.
4. Air-dry laundry instead of using the tumble dryer whenever you can.
5. Unplug chargers and switch off standby devices, or put them on a power strip you can turn off.
6. Seal drafts around windows and doors with weatherstripping or caulk.
7. Set your water heater to 120 F (49 C) and insulate the tank and hot pipes.
8. Use lids on pots and match pan size to the burner when cooking.
9. Clean or replace HVAC filters every few months so the system does not work harder than it needs to.
10. Run the dishwasher only when full and skip the heated dry cycle.
